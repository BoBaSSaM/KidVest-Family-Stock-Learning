---
name: Practice sandbox boundary
description: Product and data boundary between child practice trades and the family-approved investment flow.
---

The child practice sandbox starts with virtual funds and keeps its balance, holdings, and trade history separate from family wallets, family holdings, investment requests, rewards, and payments. Practice trades are child-only and never require parent approval. The regular family portfolio remains approval-gated. The app does not execute real share purchases.

**Why:** The user requested independent, no-approval learning trades while preserving the family's approval process; the product is educational paper trading, not a brokerage.

**How to apply:** Keep any future sandbox features confined to the child's practice account and label them as simulations. Do not route sandbox actions through the family request or payment flow.
